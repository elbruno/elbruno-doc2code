# Contact Book

## Overview
A simple console application to manage a personal contact book.
Users can add, list, search, and delete contacts.

## Actors
- **User**: manages their contacts

## Domain Entities

### Contact
- Name (text, required)
- Email (text, optional)
- Phone (text, optional)
- Category: Personal, Work, Family

## Business Rules
- BR01: Name is required and must not be empty
- BR02: At least one of Email or Phone must be provided
- BR03: Email must contain an '@' character if provided
- BR04: No two contacts may share the same Email

## State Machines
None — contacts are simply created and deleted.
